**Solmar Bay** — a sun-soaked open-world crime game that plays offline in a desktop browser from static files. Explore a 10×10-block coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the SBPD under a 5-star wanted system, run five story jobs for the fixer Mara Solis, take taxi fares, and hunt down 10 hidden Sun Medallions.

Systems: a living city with traffic and pedestrians, police pursuit cars and foot cops, pistol combat with armed rivals, a HUD with a round live minimap, golden-hour lighting, synthesized WebAudio sound (engine, sirens, gunfire, music), a start screen, and localStorage saves after every job and at the Safehouse. Everything is original — the city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound are invented for this game; nothing comes from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD/arrows to walk or drive, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to steal/enter a car or interact, Space to sprint or handbrake, H to honk, P/Esc to pause, M to mute.
