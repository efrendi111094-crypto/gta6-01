An original open-world crime game. All names, characters, places and art are invented for this game.

How to Play

W A S D / Arrows — walk or drive  ·  Mouse — aim  ·  Click — shoot
E — steal / enter a car, talk, save at the Safehouse
Space — sprint on foot, handbrake in a car  ·  H — horn
P / Esc — pause  ·  M — sound on/off

Run jobs for Mara Solis at her Marina office. Steal cars, lose the cops at the AutoSol Garage, take taxi fares, and find all 10 Sun Medallions hidden around the city. Stars mean heat — the more stars, the harder the SBPD chases you. The game saves after every job and at the Safehouse.
